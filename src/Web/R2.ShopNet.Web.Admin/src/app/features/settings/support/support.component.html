Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I reset my password? expand_more
You can reset your password from the Security settings tab. Click on "Change Password" and follow the instructions.
How do I update my profile information? expand_more
Navigate to the Profile settings tab where you can update your name and phone number information.
How do I manage email preferences? expand_more
Go to the Account settings tab to manage your email notification preferences and marketing email subscriptions.
